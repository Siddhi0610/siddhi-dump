THE PROBLEM

Your brain doesn't think in bullet points.

Thoughts arrive unfinished. They overlap. One idea reminds you of another, which reminds you about something you forgot yesterday.

And somehow you're expected to open a notes app and organize all of that into neat little boxes.

Thoughtloom works with the mess instead of asking you to clean it up first.
